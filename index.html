Why WebSiteofPhoto?

100% Private

Your photos stay on your hardware. No tracking, no data mining, and no third-party access to your precious moments.

Blazing Fast

Optimized for speed. Browse through thousands of high-resolution images instantly with smart thumbnail generation.

Responsive Design

Access your gallery from your phone, tablet, or desktop. The UI adapts perfectly to any screen size.

Simple to Deploy. Easy to Maintain.

Built with modern web technologies, this project aims to provide a "it just works" experience for users who want to host their own photo library.

  • Docker-ready for one-command deployment
  • Lightweight resource usage
  • Open source and community driven
